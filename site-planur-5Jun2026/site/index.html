Privacy Policy 🔒

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Planur is designed for students aged 13 and over. We collect only what we need, never sell your data, and never show you ads. This policy explains exactly what we collect and why.

1. Who we are

Planur is operated by Planur Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us via the Contact form on this website.

2. What data we collect and why

We collect the following when you join our waitlist or contact us:

  • First name — to address you personally when we write to you.
  • Email address — to send early access updates. Never shared with third parties for marketing.
  • Year group — to understand which age groups are most interested in Planur.
  • Country — to understand our geographic audience and comply with regional law.
  • Contact messages — content of messages sent via the Contact form, used solely to respond to your enquiry.

3. Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) — our interest in communicating with people who have expressed interest in Planur. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

4. Users under 18

Planur is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. How we store your data

Your data is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud database provider with servers in the European Union (eu-west region). Supabase is GDPR-compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We do not store payment information.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following sub-processors to operate our service:

  • Supabase Inc. — database storage (EU servers)
  • Hosting provider — website delivery

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Object — object to us processing your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any right, contact us via the Contact form. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics. No Google Analytics or similar service is used. All fonts are self-hosted — no requests are made to Google Fonts or any external font service. The only browser storage used is strictly necessary for the page to function.

9. How long we keep your data

  • Waitlist signups — kept until Planur launches and you have had the opportunity to create an account, or until you request deletion.
  • Contact messages — kept for up to 12 months after your enquiry is resolved, then deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Planur develops. If we make material changes, waitlist members will be notified by email before they take effect.

11. Contact & complaints

For privacy questions, use the Contact form on this website. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
