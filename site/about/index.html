About Planur

Planur is software for people who run instructor-led classes, so they can spend their time teaching rather than on paperwork.

Our story

Planur was started by a UK parent. Working closely with the tutors his son learns with, he felt the pain at first hand: the people doing this work were running the whole thing by hand — the register, the marks, the fees, the messages to parents — or paying a great deal for software built for far larger organisations. Neither seemed right for someone teaching a handful of pupils.

So he set out to build the tool they were missing.

Our background

Planur is built by a technical leader with many years of hands-on experience coding and architecting systems across the IT industry, including satellite telecommunications. That long experience is what Planur is built on.

Our purpose

Planur is for any teacher running classes — one-to-one tuition, small groups, art classes, music lessons, or any other kind of instructor-led teaching. Whether you teach full-time or part-time, Planur keeps your register, materials, marks and fees in one place so you can focus on teaching.

Our plans are designed to help the people most underserved today: one-to-one tutors and small-batch teachers, including university students who teach part-time. They have been handling everything manually, or paying a great deal for tools built for far larger organisations. For the same reason, we want to help play schools and nurseries manage their administration too.

The aim is to make good software reachable for all of them: simple to set up, free for a small group of students with no card, and priced for the size of your practice as it grows.

What Planur does

  • Planur for Tutors keeps a tutor's classes, register, class materials, marks and fees in one place. It can draft a practice paper with AI — which the tutor reads and edits before any student sees it.
  • The Planur student app is a planner for school students: their timetable, homework and revision.

A student can link their planner to a tutor with a code. The tutor sees the classes they teach and the results of tests they set — and nothing else. Everything else in the student's planner stays theirs.

Your data

If you are a tutor, you are the controller of your students' data and we are your processor: we use it only to run Planur for you, never for our own purposes (section 21 of our Terms). You can export your students, registers, marks and fees as spreadsheets whenever you like, without asking us. Retention is set out, with dates, in our privacy notice.

The free plan is paid for by the tutors on the paid plans — not by advertising, and nothing is taken from your register.
