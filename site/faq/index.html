Frequently asked questions

Plans, payment, the free trial, the free tools, and who is behind Planur. For how to use Planur, the Help centre walks through it step by step.

Plans and payment

Is Planur free?

There is a free plan with no card and no time limit — five students, so you can run a real class before deciding anything. You move up only as your practice grows. See pricing.

How does the free trial work?

Choose a paid plan and try it free for 14 days — no card needed, and you get the whole of that plan. When the trial ends nothing is charged and nothing is deleted: your practice simply returns to the free plan, everything you added stays, and whether you subscribe is your choice. A trial can be used once per account.

How do I pay, and what does it cost?

Paid plans are paid for by card, monthly or yearly — yearly works out about 10% cheaper — and priced in pounds. The price on the pricing page is the price you pay.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. In Planur, open Practice and plan, then Manage plan. You keep everything until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Can I have a refund?

Fees already paid are not refunded — including part-used periods. We can say that because you never have to pay to try Planur: the free plan needs no card and does not expire, and the trial needs no card either. We will always put right a billing error on our side, and we may make an exception where the circumstances warrant it — just ask. The full wording is in our terms.

What happens if I reach a plan limit, or stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Limits stop you adding another student or another upload — they never remove what is already there. Your roster, register, invoices and files stay until you delete them.

Can I pay from outside the UK?

Yes. Paid plans are available in the UK, Europe, India and Brazil, priced in local currency. The free plan and the trial work anywhere. Which countries are open is on Countries we serve, and we update it as more open.

The free tools

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email, nothing stored — they run in your browser and the answer is on the page. You do not need a Planur account. See the free tools.

Using Planur

Do I need to install anything?

No. Planur for Tutors runs in your browser, on a laptop or a phone: sign in at theplanur.co.uk/tutors/app with your email and a one-time code — there is no password. iPhone and Android apps are coming.

Can you help me get my students in?

Yes, two ways. Import a list from a spreadsheet — you see every row before anything is saved — or send it to us in whatever shape it exists and we will help. The Help centre shows where to find it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, whenever you like, without asking us: spreadsheet exports of your students, registers, marks and fees.

Can a teacher I add see my fees?

No. Teachers cannot see fees at all — that is enforced in the database, not just hidden on a screen. You invite a colleague yourself: you invite them, they accept, and you approve — three separate steps, so nobody joins your practice by accident or by guessing a link.

Who is behind Planur

Who am I trusting with my students’ data?

Planur Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17350438) and registered with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ZC275277). Read the story.

Whose data is it?

Yours. As a tutor you are the controller of your students’ data and we are your processor: we use it only to run Planur for you, never for our own purposes (section 21 of our Terms). How long we keep things is set out, with dates, in our privacy notice.

Who pays for the free plan?

The tutors on the paid plans. Not advertising, and nothing taken from your register.
