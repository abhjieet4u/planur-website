How pricing works

We set specific prices for the UK (in pounds), Europe (in euros) and India (in rupees). In other countries, prices are indicative and you are charged in your local currency at checkout — the final amount may differ slightly from the price shown in the app. Your free plan is never affected: nothing to pay, nothing to cancel, and nothing removed.

What counts is where your practice is based

When you set up, we ask which country your practice is based in — not where you happen to be today. That is what decides which school years you are offered. A UK practice that spends the winter abroad is still a UK practice.
