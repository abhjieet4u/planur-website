Getting started

Your first hour with Planur for Tutors

Everything below happens in your browser — on a laptop or a phone, no install, no setup call. Follow the six steps in order the first time, or jump to the one you need. Start free with five students, or try a paid plan free for fourteen days; neither asks for a card.

Create your practice

Open Planur in your browser and sign in with your email — we send a short code, so there is no password to remember. Give your practice a name; that is your business, not a single room, so two teaching locations still live under one practice.

You land on a Get started checklist that walks you through the first four things: a class, your students, your sessions, and inviting students to the app.

Open Planur › sign in › name your practice
2

Add a class

Create a batch — a group who study together, or a one-to-one named after the student. Give it a name like “Year 6 — 11+ Prep” and a subject, and it is ready for students and sessions.

Classes & teaching › New batch
3

Add your students

Add each student with as much or as little as you have — a name is enough to start. Parent name, email and phone are optional, and you set the billing cycle per student. A student can link their own Planur account later with a join code, but you can run everything without waiting for them to.

Students › New student
4

Your day, at a glance

The dashboard leads with what you do most — take a register, add a student, draft an AI test, or record a payment. Below that: your next class, how many registers are still to mark, your student count, and anything left unpaid.

Dashboard
5

Draft an AI practice test

Tell it what to cover, the subject, year group, difficulty and how many questions — it drafts the paper in seconds. The AI only ever sees those few words you typed. No student name, mark, register or uploaded file is sent with the request.

Tests & marks › New AI practice test
6

Read it, then publish

Every question comes with its marked answer and a worked explanation, for you to check. Nothing reaches a student until you press Publish — you read it first, always. Edit any question, or regenerate, before it goes out.

Worked example

A one-to-one tutor's week

Say you teach one-to-one at 5, 6 and 7 pm, Monday to Friday: fifteen lessons a week. Here is how that looks in Planur.

Once

  • Add your students, or import a list with the parents' details.
  • Create a one-to-one class for each student. It is named after them, and their fee sits on it.
  • Add a 60-minute session at their time and tick Repeats weekly. Twice a week? Add two.

Every week

  • The Calendar shows the week. Subscribe in your calendar puts it on your phone.
  • Take the register after each lesson: present, absent or authorised.
  • Share worksheets, and draft an AI practice test when it helps. You read it before you publish it.

Every month

  • Bill a class creates each student's invoice for the period.
  • Record payment when the money arrives, and send the receipt.
  • Overdue fees show on the Fees page, with a reminder ready to send.

A full timetable like this needs a paid plan. The 14-day free trial lets you set it all up first, with no card. Step-by-step detail is in Account › Help inside Planur.
