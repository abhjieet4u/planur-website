Free tools

Small things we needed ourselves. No sign-up, no email, nothing stored — they work in the browser and the answer is on the page.

Attendance percentage calculator

Sessions attended over sessions possible, with what the figure means against the thresholds English schools use — including why an authorised absence still counts.

How many days can you miss?

The same arithmetic the other way round. Ninety per cent of a school year sounds respectable until you hear it as four weeks.

Grade percentage calculator

Marks into a percentage, plus what is still needed on the paper to come. It will not guess at a grade, because boundaries move every year.

Random group generator

Paste a class list, get even groups. The names never leave your browser.
