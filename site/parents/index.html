The Planur parent portal

If your child's tutor uses Planur, you can see their classes, marks, attendance, materials and bills in one place — and how to pay.

Parent log in

Classes and materials

Upcoming lessons, and the worksheets, links and notes the tutor shares with your child's classes.

Marks and attendance

Each mark next to the class average, once ten or more have sat the test — never another child's mark. Students aged 16 and over can choose to keep these private.

Bills and receipts

What is due, how the tutor would like to be paid, and every confirmed payment, grouped by child.

How to get started

  1. Ask your child's tutor for a parent code. It is not the code your child uses — every child has a separate one for parents.
  2. Sign in at parent.theplanur.co.uk with your email address. We send you a code; there is no password.
  3. Enter the parent code. Your tutor approves the request, and your child's page appears. Children with more than one tutor, or brothers and sisters, can all be added to the same account.
Paying safely. Payments go straight to your tutor — Planur never handles the money. Before you pay a tutor for the first time, the portal asks you to confirm their details with them directly. It asks again whenever their payment details change. Planur and your tutor will never send you new bank details by email, text or WhatsApp: only trust what the portal shows.
